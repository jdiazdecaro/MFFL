Reading it

SOS
Average projected score of your fourteen opponents, in points per week. Above 115.6 is harder than average, below is easier. The full league spans just 11 points — about 8%.
The split
The same figure for weeks 1–7 and weeks 8–14. Green is below league average, red is above. The back half matters more, since that's when seeding is decided.
Toughest week
The week you are least likely to win, and who you face. The percentage is your chance of winning that matchup — so wk 12 · 40% means you win that week 40 times out of 100. Lower is worse; anything under 15% is shown in red.
Bye-week dip
Every NFL team gets one week off. When several of your starters sit out the same week, you're forced to fill those slots from your bench and your lineup scores less. This column shows the week that hits you hardest and how many points below your season average you fall — −34 pts means you project 34 points weaker than a normal week.

It says nothing about your opponent, which is why it differs from the column beside it for 9 of 16 teams. JPuente1 is the example: his lineup bottoms out in week 11, but he happens to face the league's weakest team that week and is still an 84% favorite.
Shift
How much the schedule changes between halves. Anything past ±8 points is a meaningfully different second season.

Worth knowing

88boyz11 drew the easiest schedule at 110.1 points per week; GerCllz drew the hardest at 121.3. The whole league fits inside 11 points — about 8% — so no one was handed or denied a season by the draw alone.

GerCllz is the league's boogeyman. Seven of the other fifteen teams have GerCllz as their single toughest week — RayRod94, TexasTimeshifts, JPuente1, NickBowens, PrimeTimeJG, CarlosMalagon and efuentesjr. Fristoe is the answer for four more. Between them, those two teams account for eleven of the fifteen worst weeks in the league. There is a neat irony in it: GerCllz is everyone else's hardest matchup and holds the toughest schedule on the board.

Fristoe drew the best-shaped schedule. Second-easiest overall, and it collapses from 120.9 to 102.0 — the softest back half by six points. A projected ten-win team gets its easiest run exactly when seeding is settled.

CarlosMalagon got the mirror image — 109.3 early, 128.9 late, the sharpest hardening on the board. If he's still alive at week 8, the schedule is what ends it.
